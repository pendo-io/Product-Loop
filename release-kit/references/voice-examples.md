# Voice Examples: Good vs. Bad

Use this file when you're uncertain about tone, when the PM asks for style calibration,
or when you want to self-check before delivering.

---

## Which voice to use

Use your company's brand voice guide. Paste or upload it when you run the skill, or fill in
"Your brand voice" below. If you have no guide, use the default voice.

### Default voice (only if you have no brand voice guide)

Clear, specific, and confident. Say what changed and why it matters to the reader, in plain
language. Show proof instead of adjectives, write to "you" more than "we", and stop when the
point is made.

### Your brand voice

Fill this in once and it replaces the default. The first-run setup (`references/setup.md`)
fills it for you and saves it as `release_kit.voice`.

- **Voice in one sentence:** [how your brand sounds]
- **We are:** [3 to 5 traits, for example direct, warm, expert]
- **We are not:** [3 to 5 traits you avoid]
- **Readers we write for:** [personas]
- **Example line that sounds like us:** [one real line from your own copy]

## About the examples

The examples below use a fictional work-tracking product with **Timesheets**, **Calendar
Sync** (which logs meeting time to the right project), **Recurring tasks**, and **Plan &
Track**. The lessons carry over to any product. If setup saved your own product examples
(`release_kit.product_examples`), calibrate against those instead.

---

## Opening lines

**Bad:**
> "In a world where work never stops, staying organized matters more than ever."

**Bad:**
> "We're delighted to unveil an exciting update to our platform."

**Bad:**
> "Modern teams face more complexity than ever before, and planning is no exception."

**Good:**
> "Friday afternoon is for finishing work, not reconstructing it for a timesheet."

**Good:**
> "Every recurring task you set up by hand is one more thing to forget when the schedule changes."

**Good:**
> "Plan & Track starts from the deadline and works back to the first task."

---

## Feature descriptions

**Bad:**
> "Our cutting-edge, intelligent solution effortlessly streamlines every workflow for maximum productivity."

**Bad:**
> "Harness actionable insights to empower your teams and accelerate success at scale."

**Good:**
> "Calendar Sync logs each meeting to its project, so your timesheet matches your calendar without retyping it."

**Good:**
> "Set a task to repeat every second Tuesday. If the schedule moves, edit it once and every future copy follows."

---

## Blog post openers

**Bad:**
> "We're pleased to share a major update that will revolutionize how you plan."

**Good:**
> "Ask five people what they worked on last Thursday and you'll get five guesses. Timesheets shouldn't run on guesses."

**Good:**
> "The weekly status meeting exists because nobody trusts the plan. Make the plan trustworthy and the meeting gets shorter."

## Calls to action

**Bad:**
> "Find out more about everything our amazing platform can do."

**Bad:**
> "Explore the possibilities with [Company] and take your productivity further."

**Good:**
> "Connect your calendar"

**Good:**
> "Book a walkthrough"

**Good:**
> "Read the setup guide"

**Good:**
> "Open your timesheet"

---

## Bullet copy

**Bad:**
- Offers unparalleled flexibility for teams of all sizes
- Empowers users with intelligent automation
- Provides an intuitive, user-friendly interface

**Good:**
- Pull meetings from your calendar into the right project
- Approve a week of entries in one pass
- Export hours by client for invoicing

## Words and phrases to avoid

| Avoid | Use instead |
|-------|------------|
| seamlessly | (just say what it does) |
| robust | strong, reliable, or describe the specific capability |
| powerful | (describe what it actually does) |
| next-level | (describe the outcome) |
| game-changing | (describe the impact) |
| innovative | (show it, don't say it) |
| leverage | use |
| utilize | use |
| unlock | (overused, only if it's truly about access) |
| unprecedented | (almost never true) |
| delve into | explore, look at |
| it's worth noting | (just say the thing) |
| in today's landscape | (just start with the insight) |
| best-in-class | (prove it or cut it) |
