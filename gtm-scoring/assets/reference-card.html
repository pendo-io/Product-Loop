GTM Launch Scoring — Reference Guide

Rate each of the four dimensions from 1 to 4; the ratings add up to a score out of 16. This rubric is a basic example: adapt the levels and tiers to your own launch process.

D1
Customer Experience ImpactHow much does this change what a user sees, understands, or does inside your product?
1Invisible

Purely backend. No user-visible change — performance, infra, or data pipeline work.

2Subtle change

Label, copy, tooltip, or calculation update, or a small addition such as a new button or export on an existing screen. Users may notice; most won't without being told.

3Meaningful update

An existing feature, screen or workflow changes noticeably. Users will remark on it.

4Net-new feature

Entirely new tool, screen, workflow, or interaction pattern. Users need to discover and adopt it.

D2
Customer Adoption ImpactHow much is this release expected to increase adoption or stickiness for this feature or product area?
1No behavior change

Edge case, bug fix, or niche workflow. Doesn't change how users engage.

2Existing users only

Some current users may utilize. Unlikely to draw anyone new to the product.

3Announcement-worthy

Worth proactively telling all existing users about. Could deepen engagement or retention.

4Brings in new users

Expected to expand who uses this feature or the feature's overall product area.

D3
Competitive Advantage / Win-RateHow much does this strengthen your competitive position or increase win rate for someone evaluating your product?
1No change

Too minor to impact win rate or competitive edge. Won't come up in competitive conversations.

2Closing a known gap

Matches what key competitors offer or removes an objection. Rarely the deciding factor in a deal.

3Clear competitive angle

Distinct story that meaningfully improves win rate in deals where you were previously losing. GTM should know how to use it.

4Leading the market

Few or no competitors have this. Expected to unlock new segments, reverse losses, or be frequently cited as the reason you won.

D4
Enablement EffortHow much new enablement work does this require across the GTM org?
1No enablement lift

Help-center update, release notes, product announcement sufficient. No demo, training, or messaging changes required.

2Just in time

Impacts value story and demo slightly. Minor updates to existing enablement but no live field time required. Covered in your regular internal update.

3Standard feature training

Need live time with some GTM segments. Feature impacts positioning/value or there's pricing nuance. Requires your enablement lead time (for example N weeks).

4Full GTM enablement

Typical of larger launches. Requires your launch training, messaging updates, and a full demo environment and script. All GTM functions and leadership aligned.

≤7 Release marketing only (Tier 3)
8–13 Needs a conversation (potential Tier 2)
14–16 Potential Tier 1 launch
